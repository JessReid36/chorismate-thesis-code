Seven pre-registered arrangements on frame 42436's own grid. Each sphere's first-order effect on the barrier is q × a + L, with a = VTS − VR the substrate's own potential difference at that point and L the sphere's Lennard-Jones energy at the transition state minus at the reactant. Negative lowers the barrier. Every design spends the same budget, Σq² ≤ 2.0 e², so more spheres means thinner charges — which is the whole story on the right.
